# Proposal: Solar + Battery Microgrid for Meadow Hall Schools

**Prepared for:** Meadow Hall Schools  
**Objective:** Cut electricity spend, improve reliability, and stabilize power quality by powering the campus through a solar + battery inverter-backed microgrid.

---

## 1) Executive Summary (The Offer)
We propose a **~250 kWp Solar PV + 300 kWh Battery + Site Inverter System** designed to:
- Supply a large portion of daytime energy directly from solar
- Use batteries to smooth solar variability (clouds/irradiance dips) and support short-duration autonomy
- Feed the site via an inverter-controlled bus for better power quality and controllability

**Financial headline (at ₦300/kWh):**
- Estimated annual savings: **~₦94m–₦113m net/year** (range depends on solar yield and uptime)
- Simple payback: **~2.4–2.9 years** (most-likely ~**2.6 years**)

---

## 2) System Size (Based on Meadow Hall Peak Demand)
From historical data, Meadow Hall’s measured peak demand reaches approximately **443 kW**.

**Proposed system (base package):**
- **Solar PV:** ~**250 kWp** (≈ **455 panels**)
- **Battery:** **300 kWh (LFP)**
- **Inverter system (site PCS/inverter):** budgeted at **₦90m** (final kW/kVA rating to be confirmed during site survey and motor-start assessment)
- **Cables/installation:** **₦20m**

> Note: The PV and battery reduce imported energy; they do not “create” energy beyond what solar provides. Battery mainly improves reliability and enables solar utilization during variability.

---

## 3) Energy & Savings (₦300/kWh)
### 3.1 Expected PV production (Lagos yield)
Assuming ~**250 kWp** installed and typical Lagos yield of **3.8–4.6 kWh/kWp/day**:
- Annual PV energy: **~347,000 – 420,000 kWh/year**

### 3.2 Savings
At **₦300/kWh** avoided cost:
- **Gross savings:** **₦104m – ₦126m / year**
- **Net savings (≈10% allowance for losses, soiling, downtime):** **₦94m – ₦113m / year**

## 5) Battery “1-Hour Autonomy” (Clarified)
The **300 kWh** battery is excellent for **solar degradation ride-through** and short-duration backup.

- At **full peak load (443 kW)**, 300 kWh provides **~0.7 hours theoretical** (less after usable DoD and losses).
- If Meadow Hall requires **a full 1 hour at the 443 kW peak**, we recommend upgrading to **~800 kWh** battery capacity.

**Recommendation:** Confirm the required autonomy definition:
- **Ride-through during solar dips** (most common) → **300 kWh is typically sufficient**
- **Full-site 1-hour autonomy at peak** → **upgrade battery capacity**
